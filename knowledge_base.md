# Mock knowledge base (school project, general information only)



## Levels

Level 0 means nothing detected. Higher levels mean more was detected. Trends over several scans matter more than one reading.



## Glucose

Urine normally has no glucose. A positive result can follow a very sugary meal. If it keeps showing up, repeat the test and talk to a doctor.



## Protein

Small amounts of protein can appear with exercise or dehydration. A reading that stays positive over several scans is worth discussing with a doctor.



## Leukocytes

Leukocytes are white blood cells. A positive reading can mean inflammation or infection in the urinary tract, especially together with nitrites.



## Nitrites

A positive nitrite reading may point to a urinary tract infection, but a negative result does not rule one out.



## Taking a good scan

Use bright, even light, place the card flat, and keep the whole card in the photo. Poor lighting is a common cause of wrong readings.
